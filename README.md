# Jan Busse

**Data Scientist · AI Engineering · Semantic Analytics · Power BI**

I build and evaluate AI and analytics systems with an emphasis on **correctness, reproducibility, explicit review, and traceable evidence**.

My work sits at the intersection of AI-agent engineering, semantic modeling, and analytics engineering. I am particularly interested in the gap between a system that *looks* correct and one that can actually demonstrate correctness.

## Featured projects

### [AI Agent Evaluation Lab](https://github.com/JanNeoV/ai-agent-evaluation-lab)
**Rigorous evaluation of analytical AI agents — beyond schema-valid JSON.**

A public research harness with separate evaluation stages, deterministic answer scoring, evidence verification, and explicit cost tracking. A documented, small Snowflake Cortex experiment illustrates why improved response structure does not necessarily imply analytical accuracy.

[Repository](https://github.com/JanNeoV/ai-agent-evaluation-lab) · [Methodology](https://github.com/JanNeoV/ai-agent-evaluation-lab/blob/main/docs/methodology.md) · [Results](https://github.com/JanNeoV/ai-agent-evaluation-lab/blob/main/docs/results.md)

### [Power BI Semantic Assurance](https://github.com/JanNeoV/pbi-osi-converter)
**Testing whether semantic-model transformations preserve business meaning.**

An evidence-led Power BI-to-Snowflake conversion audit and offline, review-controlled demonstration. The captured audit tracks **46 Power BI measures**, including **25 omitted target metrics** and reviewed translation errors. Runtime numerical equivalence is explicitly marked as unverified.

[Repository](https://github.com/JanNeoV/pbi-osi-converter) · [Evidence](https://github.com/JanNeoV/pbi-osi-converter/blob/main/docs/EVIDENCE.md) · [Limitations](https://github.com/JanNeoV/pbi-osi-converter/blob/main/docs/LIMITATIONS.md)

### [Energy Markets & Power BI](https://github.com/JanNeoV/public_energy_markets)
**Enterprise BI modeling with energy-market data.**

A Power BI case study covering electricity prices and generation, multiple fact granularities, galaxy-schema modeling, and Object-Level Security examples.

[Repository](https://github.com/JanNeoV/public_energy_markets)

### [Microsoft Fabric + dbt + Eurostat](https://github.com/JanNeoV/fabric-dbt-eurostat)
**Dimensional analytics engineering in Microsoft Fabric.**

A tutorial-derived dbt modeling example using European public data, organized into source, staging, and mart layers with declarative data-quality tests.

[Repository](https://github.com/JanNeoV/fabric-dbt-eurostat)
